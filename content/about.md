### From coding..

My developer life started at 14 when I enrolled at the [HTL Villach](https://www.htl-villach.at/) with a focus on IT. In those five years I learned to code: Assembler, C, C++, COBOL, and that new thing: Java. I continued the path at the [Technical University of Vienna](https://www.tuwien.ac.at), eventually earning my Master&apos;s Degree in Software Engineering.

In parallel to my studies, I started to work at the [Austrian Institue of Technology (AIT)](https://www.ait.ac.at), initially as part of an [EU FP7 Project on Quantum Key Distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secure_Communication_based_on_Quantum_Cryptography), later mostly on EU Horizon 2020 projects focusing on [Secure Data Storage](https://prismacloud.eu/index.html) and [Identity Management in the Cloud](https://credential.eu/).

### .. to hacking, ..

In late 2012, a friend asked me to help out in their [pen-testing company](https://www.coretec.at). I initially declined. Lured in by the offer of free coffee, I visited their office and thus began my hacking career. Over the next six years, I alternated between engineering (AIT) and pen-testing until, in 2018, hacking won and I became a full-time pen-tester.

Not forgetting my roots, over time I became more and more involved in [AppSec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application_security), Developer Trainings, and consulting roles.

### .. but still caring.

'Only' hacking feels unfulfilling: while pwning systems is fun, there must be a more constructive side to security.

To explore this aspect of mine, I taught various security lectures at the [FH/Technikum Wien](https://www.technikum-wien.at/) and became an OWASP leader, both for a local chapter (Vienna, then Klagenfurt) and a [project](https://github.blog/2021-12-06-write-more-secure-code-owasp-top-10-proactive-controls/).

This never felt enough. So in 2022, I quit my day job and enrolled in a PhD program at the Technical University of Vienna. My focus lies on using AI/ML for offensive security as well as hackers' decision making processes in general. To finance this, I have become a freelance security consultant accompaning softare devs/companies on their road towards security self-reliance. Secretly I hope to bridge academia and industry through my work.
